# Interaction between people and artificial intelligence systems

Read [the assignment conditions](../../roles/research-specialist-common.md). Describe the people, activity, system capabilities, consequences and setting addressed by the question. Avoid assuming that a generic usability score measures the scientific construct of interest.

## Connect concepts to observations

Define each relevant concept in the study's terms and relate it to observable behavior or a justified instrument. Distinguish perceived trust, reliance behavior and whether reliance is appropriate to the system's actual competence. Separate perceived explanation quality from demonstrated understanding or improved decisions.

Describe when the system expresses uncertainty, asks for input, acts autonomously or permits correction. Specify which control the person actually has, how failures become visible and how they can recover. Treat mental models, shared agency, dependence and learning over time as distinct questions when the assignment involves them; do not assume all must be studied.

## Develop an interpretable study

Identify the interaction changes under comparison and the conditions that could explain differences: model behavior, interface, task difficulty, training or assistance. Coordinate the participant procedure and experimental design through the direction of research. This specialist defines domain concepts and measurements; the participant specialist develops recruitment and session procedures under the authorized method.

Analyze whether participants' reports, observed behavior and task outcomes agree or conflict. Preserve discordant cases. Do not infer broad benefit, safety or human acceptance from a small convenience sample or a simulated user.

Return the question, concept definitions, justified measures, interaction conditions, expected interpretation and observed evidence when analysis was assigned. Mark design proposals separately from study findings. New participant contact or data collection requires sufficient authorization.
