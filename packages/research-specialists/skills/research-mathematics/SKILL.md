# Mathematical research

Read [the assignment conditions](../../roles/research-specialist-common.md). State the proposition or derivation precisely before working on it. Define mathematical objects, quantifiers, domain, regularity and boundary assumptions needed for the argument. Preserve the requested statement; do not silently replace it with a weaker or numerically convenient claim.

## Construct or inspect the argument

Identify the lemmas and established results on which the reasoning depends, including conditions needed to apply them. Track each nontrivial implication and distinguish equivalence from a one-way implication. Check operations that can change the solution set or invalidate an argument, including division by possibly zero terms, differentiation of nonsmooth objects and exchange of limits, integrals or expectations.

For existence, uniqueness, convergence or optimality claims, state which property is actually proved. Examine relevant boundary and degenerate cases. When the claim fails, provide a counterexample that satisfies the stated assumptions and identify exactly which conclusion it contradicts.

## Use computation appropriately

Symbolic systems and numerical calculations can discover structure, verify algebra in a specified domain or expose counterexamples. Record the expressions, assumptions, precision and commands used. A finite set of successful numerical checks is not a proof of a universal statement; a symbolic transformation with unstated branch conditions is not automatically a valid derivation.

Do not claim a complete proof when a required step remains conjectural. Present the proved portion, the unresolved implication and any additional assumption under which the result would follow. Proposed assumptions are decisions for Roberto when they change the scientific statement.

Return the precise statement, definitions, justified argument or counterexample, dependencies, checks and unresolved issues. For manuscript work, retain notation consistent with the supplied document unless a change is necessary and explained.
