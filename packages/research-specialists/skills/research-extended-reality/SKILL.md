# Extended reality research

Read [the assignment conditions](../../roles/research-specialist-common.md). Define the virtual, augmented or mixed experience and the question being investigated. Distinguish an experience prototype, a device measurement and a participant study; each supports different conclusions.

## Describe the experience and conditions

Document the device and software versions, tracked degrees of freedom, reference frames, interaction inputs and rendering conditions relevant to the question. For spatial tasks, describe scale, registration, viewpoint, occlusion and correspondence with the physical environment. Record whether haptic or other sensory feedback is present and how it is synchronized.

Define presence, embodiment, task performance, comfort or other assigned outcomes before selecting measurements. Do not use those constructs as interchangeable labels. Consider accessibility and alternative interaction requirements that belong to the assigned population and use case rather than imposing a universal interface.

## Evaluate the authorized question

For a rendering or tracking question, report measured timing or errors with the actual measurement setup. For a participant question, coordinate instruments, exposure and session procedures through the direction of research. Preserve any authorized stopping or comfort procedure; a prototype demonstration does not constitute participant evidence.

Record device execution separately from desktop simulation or screenshots. Distinguish limitations caused by hardware, content, tracking, task design and the proposed method when evidence supports the distinction. Simulator results cannot establish device comfort or physical-world registration accuracy.

Return the experience specification, experiment conditions, measures, source artifacts, actual observations and limitations. Building scientific prototype code is allowed within the assignment; deploying an application or operating additional equipment requires the appropriate authorization.
