# Studies with human participants

Read [the assignment conditions](../../roles/research-specialist-common.md). Start from the authorized question, population, method, materials and intended inference. Identify which elements are established and which remain decisions for Roberto.

## Prepare the study

Specify participant activities and the conditions needed to interpret them: setting, system behavior, instructions, exposure, order, duration and assistance. Separate participant-facing wording from researcher procedures. Preserve approved instruments and document modifications; do not describe a modified instrument as validated without support.

Connect each interview question, observation or questionnaire item to the concept being studied. For exploratory qualitative work, preserve the chosen epistemological and analytic approach instead of imposing a quantitative hypothesis structure. For mixed methods, explain how the qualitative and quantitative components answer the question and where they will be integrated.

Use the relevant institutional conditions already supplied or authorized for inspection. Identify consent, withdrawal, recording, access, retention and de-identification requirements that affect the actual study. A document draft is not ethics approval. Do not invent institutional requirements or seek a new approval process for work that does not require one. Report unresolved conditions before the affected activity.

## Analyze authorized materials

Preserve original records and document de-identification and transcription decisions. For interviews or observations, apply the authorized coding or interpretive procedure, retain links from interpretations to excerpts, and examine discordant cases. Do not impose intercoder agreement, saturation or a specific coding framework when it is incompatible with the agreed method.

Distinguish participant statements, researcher observations and interpretations. Report missing sessions or unusable records with reasons. For quantitative components, coordinate the specified measures and dependency structure with the statistical specialist through the direction of research.

## Deliver

Return versioned instruments, researcher procedures, the proposed or executed analysis, and the conditions needed to use them. Identify actual participant data separately from fictional examples or simulated sessions. Recruitment, contact, consent collection and new recordings remain separate operations requiring sufficient authorization. The Human–AI Interaction specialist supplies domain concepts; this skill supplies procedures for studying people.
