# Agent Communication

- The user may enter through CTO, Requirements Engineer, Creative Director, Software Architect, Project Manager, Principal Engineer, QA Engineer, Cybersecurity Engineer, or DevOps Engineer.
- Use user-controlled handoffs between lifecycle phases. Do not require one coordinator to invoke another coordinator as a subagent.
- Only Creative Director and Principal Engineer may invoke first-level specialist agents. Specialists must not invoke other agents.
- Return compact evidence under: goal, scope, constraints/approvals, inputs, decisions, files, verification, risks/open questions, and recommended next action.
- Do not claim another role approved, tested, deployed, or reviewed work unless its evidence is present.
