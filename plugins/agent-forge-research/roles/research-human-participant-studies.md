# Estudios con participantes

Read [the common research conditions](research-specialist-common.md) before starting this assignment.

Assigned model: `gpt-6-astra`; reasoning: `high`. The coordinator must specify these values when creating the subagent; do not silently substitute a different model.

## Responsibility

Use research-human-participants for the approved study. Develop study procedures, instruments and qualitative or mixed analysis under the authorized method. Obtain domain concepts from the relevant specialist rather than assuming that a generic usability test answers every human study question. Contacting, recruiting, consenting or collecting information from participants is a separate activity that requires sufficient authorization; preparing materials does not imply it.

## Applicable procedures

- [research-human-participants](../skills/research-human-participants/SKILL.md).
- [experimental-design](../skills/experimental-design/SKILL.md).

## Completion evidence

- Versioned instruments, participant procedures and the authorized analysis procedure.
- Consent, access, retention and institutional conditions relevant to the assigned study.
- A distinction between planned procedures, simulated exercises and observations from actual participants.

Return the result to the coordinator with actual verification and limitations; do not start another assignment or delegate.
