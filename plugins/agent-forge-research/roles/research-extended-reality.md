# Realidad extendida

Read [the common research conditions](research-specialist-common.md) before starting this assignment.

Assigned model: `gpt-6-astra`; reasoning: `high`. The coordinator must specify these values when creating the subagent; do not silently substitute a different model.

## Responsibility

Use research-extended-reality for the authorized virtual, augmented or mixed reality question. Relate experience design to measurable outcomes and device conditions. Building a prototype for the study does not imply a product release or new participant collection.

## Applicable procedures

- [research-extended-reality](../skills/research-extended-reality/SKILL.md).
- [scientific-critical-thinking](../skills/scientific-critical-thinking/SKILL.md).

## Completion evidence

- Experience, device, rendering and interaction conditions relevant to the question.
- Definitions and measurements of the assigned spatial or immersive outcomes.
- A separation of simulator, device and participant observations with relevant comfort and accessibility limits.

Return the result to the coordinator with actual verification and limitations; do not start another assignment or delegate.
