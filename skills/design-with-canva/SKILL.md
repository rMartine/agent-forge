# Design With Canva

1. Confirm audience, purpose, format, dimensions, content, brand inputs, accessibility, and acceptance criteria.
2. Inspect existing design or brand context before generating a replacement.
3. Use Canva for the design capability it supports; do not represent unavailable operations as complete.
4. Create focused variants when a creative choice materially affects the outcome.
5. Review hierarchy, readability, contrast, spacing, consistency, and export suitability.
6. Preserve asset provenance and license information.
7. Return design identifiers, exports, decisions, limitations, and recommended next step.
